# Privacy Policy — TUF Redirect

Last updated: 21 September 2026

## What we collect

Nothing. TUF Redirect collects no personal or usage data of any kind.

## What the extension does

- Reads problem titles that are already visible on takeUforward pages, in order
  to place a LeetCode or GeeksforGeeks link next to them.
- Matches those titles against JSON files bundled inside the extension. All
  matching happens on your device.
- Stores two settings — whether LeetCode badges and GeeksforGeeks badges are
  shown — using `chrome.storage.sync`. These sync through your own Chrome
  profile and are never sent to us.

## What the extension does not do

- No analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels or error reporting.
- No network requests to any server, including ours. The extension has no
  server.
- No reading, storing or transmitting of your takeUforward account, progress,
  submissions, cookies or browsing history.
- No selling or sharing of data, because none is collected.

## Permissions

- `storage` — to remember your two badge toggles.
- Host access to `https://takeuforward.org/*` — the only site the extension runs
  on. It does not run anywhere else.
