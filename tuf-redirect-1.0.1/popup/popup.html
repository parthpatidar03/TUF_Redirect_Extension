How it works

  1. Open takeuforward.org/practice/dsa.
  2. An External Link column appears in the table, next to Resources.
  3. Click a link to open that problem on the judge. Nothing else changes.

A solid link goes straight to the problem. A dashed one searches that site, used when the problem has no counterpart there.
